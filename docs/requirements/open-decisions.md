# Nordhaven Commerce Open Decisions

## Purpose and Decision Policy

This register contains unresolved business and operational choices. An entry remains **Open** until
the authorized decision owner records the selected option, rationale, consequences, and approval
date. Recommendations and assumptions in other documents do not close a decision.

Stable IDs must not be reused. If a decision is superseded, retain its record, mark it
**Superseded**, and link to the replacement ID. The project constitution requires unresolved items
to have an owner or decision authority and a resolution point before dependent work is released.
Named individuals are not yet supplied, so the roles below are proposed decision authorities and
must be assigned during project planning.

## Resolved stock consistency issue

HC-DEC-017 was approved on 2026-10-20 and Constitution Principle V amended. FIXED goods are independently stocked through audited assembly/disassembly; DYNAMIC bundles remain component-derived. HC-DEC-004 remains open and reservations remain ADMIN-only.

## Recorded interpretation for HC-DEC-018

HC-DEC-018 is **Open**: the form, window, and stacking rules of a direct product discount are not
approved. Milestone 06 implemented and recorded the following conservative interpretation, which the
Product Owner may widen or narrow without a data migration:

- A direct product discount is a **percentage** of the product's base price, stored as a whole number
  of basis points, with one discount per product.
- There is **no validity window**, no coupon, no tier, no campaign, no stacking, and no other
  promotion form, because HC-CAT-009 approves no broader promotion model.
- The effective price is the base price reduced by the discount, with the discounted amount rounded
  **half up** to the nearest whole minor unit, applied once, at the effective-price boundary. The
  stored effective price is constrained by the database to that same expression.
- A percentage was chosen over a fixed amount because it needs no exchange rate - HC-DEC-009 and
  HC-DEC-008 remain open - and because it stays valid when the base price changes.

Currency conversion and display-currency quotes remain unimplemented, because the exchange-rate
policy (HC-DEC-009) and the settlement currency (HC-DEC-008) are unresolved.

## Recorded interpretation for HC-DEC-019

HC-DEC-019 is **Open**: the cart lifecycle is not approved. Milestone 07 implemented and recorded the
following conservative interpretation, which the Product Owner may widen or narrow without a data
migration:

- A customer has **one cart**, enforced by a unique constraint rather than by convention. There is
  no guest cart, so there is no guest-cart merge to perform.
- A cart **does not expire**. Nothing removes it, so a customer can return to it at any time.
- A cart is **not shared** between sessions, but it _is_ shared between a customer's devices,
  because it is scoped to the account rather than to a session.
- A cart is an **intent record**, not a commitment: it never reserves or holds stock, and it creates
  no checkout, order, or payment. The reservation timing stays open under HC-DEC-004.

The cart reports both the price the customer last agreed to and the current effective price, and
says whether they differ, so the pricing revalidation HC-COM-001 requires is visible before the
checkout boundary is decided.

## Recommended Decision Sequence

This ordering is a recommendation, not an approval:

1. Resolve HC-DEC-001, HC-DEC-005, and HC-DEC-016 to establish release, jurisdiction, and operating
   boundaries.
2. Apply approved HC-DEC-017 and amended Principle V to stock operations; returns remain outside M05.
3. Resolve HC-DEC-004 and HC-DEC-006 through HC-DEC-009 together because checkout, tax, payment,
   settlement, and FX rules affect the same order totals and state transitions.
4. Resolve HC-DEC-010 through HC-DEC-015 before implementing post-order, fulfillment, and pre-order
   customer journeys.
5. Resolve HC-DEC-002 and HC-DEC-003 before releasing identity and customer-data lifecycle flows.
